# MajesDisk 1.0.0 — See what's filling your Mac

The first release of MajesDisk: a free, open-source, fully local disk analyzer for macOS, in the spirit of DaisyDisk. It has no account, no telemetry and no subscription.

## Highlights

- **Interactive ring map.** Each ring is one level deeper and each wedge is as wide as it is big. Click to go in, click the center to go back, and press Space for Quick Look.
- **Fast, honest scanning.** Parallel `getattrlistbulk` reads give you about 1.3 million files in ~40 s on an SSD. Sizes are the real space on disk, with hard links and APFS clones counted once.
- **Hidden space explained.** Space a scan can't see is broken down into purgeable space, other volumes and the rest.
- **Safe cleanup.** You drag items to the Collector and press Delete, then a 5-second countdown gives you a chance to cancel. System folders, account homes and volume roots are always refused, and there's an optional Move to Trash mode.

## Beyond DaisyDisk

- **Largest.** The 200 biggest files anywhere under a folder, filterable by kind (videos, archives & disk images, apps) and by age (unchanged for 6 months or a year).
- **Suggestions.** Known regenerable space hogs, each with a plain-language note and one-click Collect: Xcode build data, simulators, caches, iPhone backups, Docker, Trash, npm/Gradle/CocoaPods caches and `node_modules`.
- **Remembers your scans.** Results reopen instantly after a relaunch, and a rescan shows what grew since last time, with a *By growth* sort.
- **Background scans.** Each scan runs in its own disk or folder row with a percentage and time left. Several can run at once.
- **Notices outside changes.** Files deleted in Finder are struck out, and *Rescan Changed* re-measures only the folders that changed.
- **Multi-select.** Shift-click rows to collect, drag or reveal several at once.

## Also included

- Disk overview with live gauges. Hold ⌥ to count purgeable space as used.
- Scan and Delete as Administrator. macOS asks for your password each time, and nothing stays installed.
- Local Time Machine snapshots, and reclaiming purgeable space.
- Full Disk Access guidance, dark and light mode, VoiceOver labels and Reduce Motion support.
- DaisyDisk-style keyboard shortcuts and trackpad pinch to enter and leave folders.

## Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

## Install

This release is source-only, with no signed download yet. You only need the Command Line Tools; Xcode isn't required.

```bash
xcode-select --install
git clone https://github.com/yegorglz/Majesdisk.git
cd Majesdisk
scripts/build-app.sh release
open dist/MajesDisk.app
```

For complete results, grant Full Disk Access when the app asks: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access. Then relaunch MajesDisk.

## Known limitations

- No signed or notarized download yet.
- No cloud-drive scanning, and no snapshot size estimates.
- Builds signed without a fixed identity need Full Disk Access granted again after each rebuild. Use `MAJESDISK_SIGN_IDENTITY` to avoid this.
- Scans of the same spinning hard disk run in parallel instead of queueing.
